Limbu Cultural Festival 2026 Announced

Annual cultural festival to be held on 15 September 2026 at the district headquarters

The Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee is pleased to announce the annual Limbu Cultural Festival 2026, scheduled to be held on 15 September 2026 at the district headquarters in Diphu. The festival is a celebration of the rich cultural heritage of the Limbu community and is open to all community members.

The Cultural Secretary, Narayan Subba (Khapung), informed that the festival will feature a variety of cultural programs, including traditional Limbu music, dance performances, folk songs, and cultural exhibitions. Community members are encouraged to participate and showcase their talents.

This year's festival will also include special segments for youth and children, ensuring that the younger generation remains connected to their cultural roots. Workshops on Limbu language and traditional crafts will be organized alongside the main cultural events.

The committee has formed a dedicated organizing team to ensure the smooth conduct of the festival. Community members are requested to register their participation with the committee office or through their respective branch committees.

The Limbu Cultural Festival is an important event in the community calendar and serves as a platform for strengthening community bonds, preserving cultural traditions, and promoting the Limbu identity. All community members are warmly invited to attend and celebrate together.
