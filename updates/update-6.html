Limbu Youth Conference 2026

First-ever youth conference paves the way for future youth engagement

The first Limbu Youth Conference was successfully held on 5 July 2026 at the Haflong Town Hall. The conference was organized by the Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee to bring together young Limbu community members and discuss issues affecting the youth, as well as to plan future initiatives for youth empowerment.

The conference was inaugurated by the President, Bal Bahadur Subba (Loksom), who emphasized the importance of youth participation in community development. He urged the young attendees to take an active role in preserving Limbu culture and traditions while also contributing to the socio-economic progress of the community.

Over 150 youth participants from various parts of Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao districts attended the conference. The event featured panel discussions, workshops, and interactive sessions on topics such as education, employment opportunities, cultural preservation, and leadership development.

The General Secretary, Subash Subba (Kerung), announced the formation of a Youth Wing under the district committee to ensure sustained engagement with the youth. The Youth Wing will be responsible for organizing events, skill development programs, and advocacy initiatives for the benefit of young Limbu community members.

The conference concluded with a resolution to hold such gatherings annually and to establish a youth network across the district. The committee expressed its commitment to supporting youth-led initiatives and providing a platform for young voices to be heard.
