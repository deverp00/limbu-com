About Us

Assam Limbu Mahasabha — Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is the official representative body of the Limbu community residing in the districts of Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao, Assam. Established to safeguard the cultural, social, and economic interests of the Limbu people, the committee serves as a vital link between the community and the larger society.

Our organization is deeply rooted in the values of unity, mutual respect, and collective progress. We work tirelessly to preserve and promote the rich cultural heritage of the Limbu community, including our language, traditions, festivals, and way of life. Through various initiatives, we aim to empower our community members and ensure their well-being and development.

History & Background

The Limbu community, also known as the Yakthung, is one of the indigenous communities of the Himalayan region, with a rich history and a distinct cultural identity. The Assam Limbu Mahasabha was formed to provide a unified platform for the Limbu people in Assam, particularly in the hill districts of Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao, where a significant Limbu population resides.

Over the years, the Mahasabha has played a pivotal role in addressing the concerns of the Limbu community, including issues related to land rights, education, employment, and cultural preservation. The district committee, as the local representative body, works closely with the community to ensure that their voices are heard and their rights are protected.

The committee is committed to fostering a sense of belonging and pride among the Limbu people, while also promoting harmonious coexistence with other communities in the region. Our efforts are guided by a vision of a just and inclusive society where every individual can thrive.

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Mission

To preserve, promote, and protect the cultural heritage, language, and traditions of the Limbu community in Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao districts, while ensuring the social, economic, and educational development of the community.

Vision

To build a united, empowered, and culturally vibrant Limbu community that contributes meaningfully to the socio-economic development of the region, while maintaining its unique identity and traditions.

Objectives

  • Preserve and promote Limbu language and culture.
  • Address community issues and advocate for rights.
  • Empower community members through education and awareness.
  • Foster unity and solidarity among the Limbu people.
  • Build partnerships with other communities and organizations.

Identity & Core Values

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha is built upon a foundation of strong cultural identity and community values. We believe in the power of unity, mutual respect, and collective action to bring about positive change. Our core values guide everything we do, from our day-to-day operations to our long-term strategic initiatives.

Our Committee

The Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee is composed of dedicated individuals who work tirelessly for the welfare of the Limbu community. The committee is led by the President and includes a Working President, several Vice Presidents, a General Secretary, and other office bearers who oversee various portfolios.

Each member brings their unique skills and expertise to the committee, ensuring that the diverse needs of the community are addressed effectively. The committee meets regularly to discuss issues, plan initiatives, and review progress.
