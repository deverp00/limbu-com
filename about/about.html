About Us

Assam Limbu Mahasabha — Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha is the apex representative body of the Limbu community in the state of Assam. The Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee serves as the official unit responsible for the welfare, cultural preservation, and overall development of the Limbu people residing in these two hill districts.

Our committee is deeply committed to upholding the rich cultural heritage, traditions, and language of the Limbu community. We work in close coordination with the state and central authorities to address the socio-economic and educational needs of our people, while fostering unity, peace, and mutual respect among all communities.

Our History

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha was established with the vision of uniting the Limbu community across Assam. The Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee was formed to specifically address the unique challenges and opportunities faced by the Limbu people in these two districts, which are home to a significant Limbu population.

Over the years, the committee has grown into a strong, democratic institution that represents the aspirations of the Limbu community. It has played a pivotal role in advocating for the rights of the Limbu people, promoting education, preserving cultural identity, and contributing to the overall development of the region.

Our history is a testament to the resilience, unity, and progressive spirit of the Limbu people. We continue to build upon this legacy, working towards a brighter and more inclusive future for all.

Purpose & Objectives

Guiding principles of the district committee

Cultural Preservation

To preserve, protect, and promote the rich Limbu culture, language, traditions, and heritage for future generations.

Community Welfare

To work for the social, economic, and educational upliftment of the Limbu community in Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao.

Unity & Harmony

To foster unity, mutual understanding, and harmonious coexistence among the Limbu people and with other communities in the region.

Youth Empowerment

To empower the younger generation through education, skill development, and leadership opportunities.

Advocacy & Rights

To advocate for the constitutional and legal rights of the Limbu community and to represent their interests at all levels of governance.

Development

To contribute to the overall socio-economic development of Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao districts through community-led initiatives.

Our Vision

To build a vibrant, progressive, and culturally rooted Limbu community in Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao, where every individual has equal opportunities to thrive while preserving their unique identity and heritage.

Our Mission

  • To serve as the unified voice of the Limbu community in the district.
  • To promote and preserve Limbu culture, language, and traditions.
  • To work towards the educational and economic development of the community.
  • To foster social harmony and cooperation with all communities.
  • To empower women, youth, and marginalized sections of the community.

Identity & Background

The Limbu people of Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao

The Limbu people are one of the indigenous communities of the Indian subcontinent, with a rich and ancient history. In Assam, the Limbu community has been an integral part of the social and cultural fabric, particularly in the hill districts of Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao.

Our identity is deeply rooted in our language, religion, customs, and traditional practices. The Limbu script, known as "Sirijonga," is a unique and important aspect of our cultural heritage. The community is known for its close-knit family structures, agricultural traditions, and vibrant festivals such as "Limbu New Year" and "Chasok Tangnam."

The Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee is proud to represent this rich heritage and works diligently to ensure that the Limbu identity is preserved and celebrated for generations to come.
