District Committee

Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee — Office Bearers & Executive Members

The District Committee of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha for Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao is composed of dedicated individuals who work tirelessly for the welfare and development of the Limbu community. The committee is led by the President and supported by a team of office bearers and executive members.

Below is the complete list of office bearers and executive committee members for the current term.

Executive Committee

The governing body of the district committee

The Executive Committee consists of all office bearers listed above, along with additional members who contribute to the governance and decision-making of the district committee. The committee meets regularly to discuss and decide on matters related to community welfare, cultural activities, and organizational development.
