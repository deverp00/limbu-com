Our Committee

Office Bearers & Executive Committee — Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee

The Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee is composed of dedicated and committed individuals who work tirelessly for the welfare and progress of the Limbu community. The committee is led by the President and includes a Working President, several Vice Presidents, a General Secretary, and other office bearers who oversee various portfolios.

Each member brings their unique skills, experience, and expertise to the committee, ensuring that the diverse needs of the community are addressed effectively. The committee meets regularly to discuss issues, plan initiatives, and review the progress of ongoing projects.

Executive Committee

The Executive Committee comprises the office bearers listed above and other appointed members who assist in the smooth functioning of the district committee. The Executive Committee meets regularly to deliberate on important matters affecting the Limbu community and to formulate strategies for community development.

The committee works through various sub-committees that focus on specific areas such as culture, education, health, youth affairs, and women's empowerment. Each sub-committee is led by a convenor and includes members with relevant expertise.

Note: Detailed Executive Committee member list and sub-committee information will be updated soon. For any queries, please contact the General Secretary.

Branches

The Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee has established branch committees in various localities to ensure effective outreach and representation at the grassroots level. These branches work closely with the district committee to implement programs and address local community concerns.
