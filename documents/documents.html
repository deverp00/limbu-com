Constitution

The constitution of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is the foundational document that governs the functioning of the committee. It outlines the objectives, structure, roles and responsibilities, election procedures, and all other aspects of the organization.

All members and office bearers are expected to abide by the provisions of the constitution. The document is available for reference and download below.
