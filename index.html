Welcome

About Our Sabha

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is a community organization dedicated to the welfare, progress, and cultural preservation of the Limbu community in the region. Established with the vision of unity and development, we work tirelessly to address the needs and aspirations of our people.

Our committee serves as a bridge between the community and the administration, ensuring that the voices of the Limbu people are heard and their rights are protected. We organize cultural events, educational programs, and social initiatives to strengthen our community bonds.
