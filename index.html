Welcome to Assam Limbu Mahasabha

Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is the official representative body of the Limbu community in the districts of Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao, Assam. We are dedicated to the preservation, promotion, and protection of Limbu culture, language, traditions, and identity.

Our committee works tirelessly to address the social, cultural, educational, and economic concerns of the Limbu people. We strive to foster unity, mutual respect, and collective progress among our community members while maintaining harmonious relations with other communities in the region.

Become a Member

Join the Assam Limbu Mahasabha and be part of our community

Eligibility

Any Limbu individual residing in Karbi Anglong or Dima Hasao district is eligible for membership.

Benefits

Members gain access to community events, welfare programs, and a network of fellow Limbu people.
