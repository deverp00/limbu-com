Membership

Become a part of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha community

Overview

Membership Introduction

Membership in the Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is open to all individuals of Limbu origin who reside in or have strong ties to the district. Our members are the backbone of the organization, contributing to the cultural, social, and economic development of the Limbu community.

By becoming a member, you join a vibrant community dedicated to preserving our heritage, advocating for our rights, and building a better future for generations to come. Your participation strengthens our collective voice and enables us to serve the community more effectively.

Who Can Join

Eligibility Criteria

Individual Membership

  • Must be of Limbu origin
  • Age 18 years or above
  • Residing in Karbi Anglong or Dima Hasao
  • Commitment to community values

Family Membership

  • Open to immediate family members
  • Spouse and children under 18
  • Same benefits as individual
  • Single membership fee
Categories

Membership Types

Choose the membership category that best suits your association with the Sabha.

Regular Member

Standard membership for individuals who actively participate in Sabha activities and contribute to the community.

Annual Fee

Life Member

One-time membership for individuals committed to long-term association with the Sabha. Lifetime voting rights and benefits.

One-Time Fee

Patron Member

Honorary membership for individuals who have made significant contributions to the Limbu community and the Sabha.

By Nomination
Advantages

Membership Benefits

What you gain by becoming a member of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha.

Voting Rights

Participate in the democratic process of the Sabha by electing office bearers and influencing key decisions.

Community Network

Connect with fellow Limbu community members, build relationships, and strengthen community bonds.

Educational Support

Access to scholarships, educational programs, and skill development workshops for members and their families.

Cultural Engagement

Participate in cultural events, festivals, and programs that celebrate and preserve Limbu heritage.

Guidelines

Membership Rules

  • All members shall abide by the constitution and bylaws of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha.
  • Members are expected to actively participate in the activities and programs of the Sabha.
  • Membership fees are to be paid on time as per the prescribed schedule.
  • Members shall uphold the dignity, unity, and reputation of the community and the organization.
  • Membership may be terminated for violation of the constitution or for activities detrimental to the Sabha.
  • Members have the right to voice their opinions and concerns through proper channels.
Requirements

Required Documents

Membership Form

Duly filled and signed membership application form.

Identity Proof

Valid government-issued identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.).

Address Proof

Proof of residence in Karbi Anglong or Dima Hasao district.

Download Membership Form
How to Join

Membership Process

  1. Step 1: Download the membership application form from the Documents section or collect it from the committee office.
  2. Step 2: Fill in the form with accurate details and attach the required documents.
  3. Step 3: Submit the completed form along with the membership fee to the committee office or any designated office bearer.
  4. Step 4: Your application will be reviewed by the committee for verification and approval.
  5. Step 5: Upon approval, you will receive your membership certificate and be formally inducted into the Sabha.
