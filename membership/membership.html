Membership

Join the Assam Limbu Mahasabha — Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee

Membership in the Assam Limbu Mahasabha is open to all Limbu individuals residing in the districts of Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao. By becoming a member, you become a part of a vibrant community committed to preserving and promoting Limbu culture, language, and traditions, while also working towards the socio-economic development of the community.

The Mahasabha provides a platform for members to actively participate in community initiatives, voice their concerns, and contribute to decision-making processes that affect the community. We welcome you to join us in our mission.

Eligibility & Membership Types

Eligibility

  • Any individual of Limbu ethnicity.
  • Must be a resident of Karbi Anglong or Dima Hasao district.
  • Minimum age of 18 years for full membership.
  • Youth membership available for individuals aged 12–18 years.
  • Commitment to the objectives and values of the Mahasabha.

Membership Types

  • Regular Member: Open to all eligible adults.
  • Youth Member: For individuals aged 12–18 years.
  • Life Member: One-time enrollment for lifetime membership.
  • Patron Member: Special category for supporters and donors.
  • Honorary Member: Conferred upon distinguished individuals.

Membership Benefits

Community Participation

Active involvement in community events, cultural programs, and decision-making.

Cultural Preservation

Access to programs promoting Limbu language, traditions, and heritage.

Welfare & Support

Access to community welfare programs, scholarships, and assistance schemes.

Networking

Connect with fellow Limbu community members across the district and beyond.

Information Access

Stay updated with official notices, circulars, and community news.

Voting Rights

Eligibility to vote in Mahasabha elections and participate in decision-making.

Requirements & How to Join

Requirements

  • Completed membership application form.
  • Copy of valid identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, etc.).
  • Passport-sized photograph (2 copies).
  • Nominal membership fee as applicable.
  • For life membership, one-time payment.
  • Youth members require parent/guardian consent.

How to Join

  1. Download the membership form from our Documents section.
  2. Fill in the required details accurately.
  3. Attach the necessary documents and photograph.
  4. Submit the completed form along with the fee to the committee office.
  5. Alternatively, you can email the scanned documents to membership@assamlimbumahasabha.org.
  6. After verification, your membership will be confirmed within 7 working days.
